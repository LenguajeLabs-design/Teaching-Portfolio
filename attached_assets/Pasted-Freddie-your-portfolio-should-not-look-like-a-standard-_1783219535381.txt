Teaching Philosophy

This should be one of your strongest pages.

Suggested title:

Language Support Should Be Visible, Practical, and Human

Core beliefs:

* Multilingual learners are not “behind.” They are building language, identity, confidence, and academic access at the same time.
* EAL support should connect students, families, classroom teachers, and school systems.
* Home languages are assets, not obstacles.
* Good scaffolding does not water down thinking. It opens the door to participation.
* AI should reduce teacher friction, not replace teacher judgment.
* Families deserve clear explanations of what language development looks like.

This page can include a few short “belief cards” instead of one long essay.

5. Project Card Template

Every project should follow the same structure. This will make your portfolio feel polished and professional.

For each project:

Project Name
One-sentence summary
Problem
Solution
My Role
Tools Used
Impact or Potential Impact
Screenshots / Link

Example:

Multilingual Learner Family Guide

A family-facing tool designed to help parents understand WIDA levels, language growth, and practical ways to support multilingual learners at home.

Problem

Families often receive language assessment information without enough explanation. WIDA levels can feel technical, confusing, or disconnected from what students actually need at home and in school.

Solution

I designed a clear, accessible guide that explains language development in family-friendly language. The guide includes level explanations, support tips, and multilingual access so families can better understand their child’s EAL journey.

My Role

I designed the concept, wrote the content, structured the user experience, and built the prototype using AI-assisted development tools.

Tools Used

Replit, GitHub, AI-assisted coding tools, WIDA-informed planning resources

Impact

This project helps make language development more transparent for families and creates a stronger bridge between school support and home understanding.
